# Calculation rules and sources - r19

Verified on 14 September 2026. The application uses official INPS rules for 26 annual working days of domestic-worker vacation, monthly twelfths and the >=15-day full-month rule; INPS rules for thirteenth-month pay; and post-1990 TFR annual accrual based on annual useful compensation / 13.5. Prior-year TFR balances require the statutory 1.5% + 75% ISTAT FOI revaluation. The courtesy income certification is not an electronically filed Certificazione Unica.

Official references are documented in the Italian version `LEGAL-RULES.it.md`.

## Thirteenth salary and contributions/tax estimates

The thirteenth salary is calculated as one twelfth of useful annual remuneration recorded in the application. When both INPS position and employment-contract number are present, reports also show estimated INPS contributions, separating employer and worker economic shares.

The private household employer is normally not an Italian withholding agent. Any IRPEF amount shown is therefore only an estimate of national gross income tax for the recorded employment income, not a final tax settlement.
