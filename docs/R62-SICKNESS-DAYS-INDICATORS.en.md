# Sickness days, employer-favour treatment and indicators - r62

r62 restores the **Sickness** label and records sickness exclusively in **calendar days**, without clock times. The effective-dated contractual engine selects the rule valid for the recorded date. For encoded domestic-work agreements from 2013 onward, job protection is 10, 45 or 180 days according to seniority (increased by 50% for documented oncological illness), while contractual paid sickness is limited to 8, 10 or 15 days: 50% through the third consecutive day and 100% from the fourth day onward.

The employer may voluntarily grant more favourable treatment and pay sickness days or permit hours beyond the contractual ceiling. The excess is tracked separately and does not inflate the remaining statutory/contractual entitlement.

Every overview indicator includes an icon. Indicators with a limit use progressive visual states (normal, warning, near/reached limit) based on the value-to-limit ratio.
